A repeatable loop

Learn it. Type it. Break it. Fix it.

Read & predict

Read the module README. Before running a command, write down what you expect it to do.

Do the work yourself

Type the labs, finish assignments, and take the quiz closed-book. Avoid copy-paste until you understand the steps.

Verify with evidence

Compare with the solutions, record what surprised you, and link your capstone evidence from your own fork.

New to the repo?

Fork it, clone your fork, run scripts/doctor.sh, then follow the roadmap from Module 00. The curriculum is designed to accompany the freeCodeCamp Docker course; it is an independent companion and is not affiliated with freeCodeCamp or the instructor.
